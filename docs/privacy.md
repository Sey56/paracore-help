# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

Paracore runs on your computer. Your scripts and your Revit model data stay there.

## What stays local

- **Your scripts** are files in the folders you choose. They are not uploaded.
- **Revit data** is read and changed locally by the add-in inside Revit. No geometry, parameters or schedules are sent anywhere.
- **Local settings** — presets, logs and similar small files — are stored in `%AppData%/Roaming/paracore-data/data/` on your machine.
- The small connector service the desktop app starts runs only on your machine. It passes scripts and results between the app and Revit; it is not a cloud service.

Paracore does not track how you use it, and it does not collect personal data.

## If you use an AI assistant

Paracore-Gen is optional and is not installed by default. If you choose to connect an AI assistant, whatever you send to that assistant is handled under **that provider's** privacy policy. Paracore itself does not send your model data to a server. You see and approve the code before it runs.

This site links to GitHub and YouTube. Those services have their own privacy policies; we are not responsible for them.
